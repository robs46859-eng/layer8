# Product Roadmap Notes

Use this when the user wants product ideas, a roadmap, or a backlog for an AI gateway.

## Good Initial Product Directions

- enterprise AI gateway
- compliance proxy
- multi-tenant AI platform backend
- cost optimization layer
- internal AI app platform

## Strong Early Wedges

- compliance and auditability
- cost control
- provider abstraction and failover
- tenant governance

## 90-Day Bias

### Month 1

- control-plane CRUD
- real provider support
- usage metering
- deployment docs
- secret handling

### Month 2

- policy engine
- failover
- RBAC
- observability
- worker reliability

### Month 3

- onboarding
- examples
- packaging
- positioning
- pilot support

## Default Packaging

- open source core
- team edition
- enterprise edition

## Backlog Conversion

Every roadmap item should become:

- title
- priority
- area
- goal
- acceptance criteria

Avoid vague backlog items such as "improve platform" or "make production ready."
